# The World in One Day — 26 November 2026

One image summing up Thursday 26 November 2026's world news, as a newspaper
front page with real, legible headlines — no AI-generated text.

## The stories in the image

- **Trade war** — Trump unveils new tariffs on 14 trading partners (India up to 25%, China capped at 20%).
- **Ukraine** — Russian missiles strike a Kyiv shopping centre; seven wounded, grain ships hit near Odesa.
- **Gaza** — Israeli strikes kill nine, including the Hamas-appointed deputy education minister.
- **Egypt** — A fuel tanker explodes on Cairo's ring road in Giza; 31 killed.
- **Red Sea** — Houthis strike Saudi Arabia's Jizan port and Ras Tanura refinery.
- **Also today** — North Korea's nuclear expansion, the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving parade postponed by a storm, a gold mine flood in Bogor (13 dead, 13 missing), a FedEx jet overrunning the runway in Miami, and UK net migration falling to 77,000.

## How the image was made

`frontpage.html` is the front page itself — plain HTML and CSS, written by hand.
It was rendered to `entry.png` (1024×1024) in headless Chrome. `main.ts` checks the
image is under the 1 MiB cap and copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
